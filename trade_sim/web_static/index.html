trade-sim
Educational use only. This simulator does not give buy or sell advice. Past performance does not predict future results.

Practise buy, hold and sell decisions

You start on a random date in the past with $10,000 and replay the market one trading day at a time. You can only see prices up to the current day.

  • Market orders fill at the next trading day's open, a little worse than the open (half bid-ask spread + slippage), plus a flat fee per order.
  • Dividends arrive on the ex-dividend date, minus US withholding tax.
  • Every trade asks for your reason and what would prove you wrong. Both go into journal.csv.
  • You get a warning when a position falls past the loss limit. Nothing is ever sold for you.
